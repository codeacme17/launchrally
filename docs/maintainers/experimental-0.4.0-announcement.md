# LaunchRally 0.4.0 — Phase 1 Experimental

LaunchRally 0.4.0 publishes the first Phase 1 candidate on npm's non-stable
`experimental` channel. It adds Product Intent, Provider-neutral Architecture,
bounded Handoff, and fresh environment-bound assurance.

Phase 1 is now **Product Complete** after independent external verification of
the exact published CLI, Codex, and Claude journeys. The release remains
**Experimental**, **not P1 Validated**, and **not Stable**. Product completion,
publication, CI, downloads, and elapsed time do not imply field validation or
Stable promotion; those remain separate future decisions.

Phase 0 remains independently Product Complete, P0 Validated, and Stable at
0.3.2. npm `latest` continues to resolve to 0.3.2; 0.4.0 is available only from
`experimental` or by exact version.

Review the [0.4.0 changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md), [migration and failed-release
guidance](p1-migration-notes.md), and [external verification
record](experimental-0.4.0-p1-evidence.md) before adoption. The signed aggregate
links the [successful publication workflow](https://github.com/codeacme17/launchrally/actions/runs/31885222828),
[v0.4.0 prerelease](https://github.com/codeacme17/launchrally/releases/tag/v0.4.0),
and [independent review](https://github.com/codeacme17/launchrally/issues/141#issuecomment-5302650515).
