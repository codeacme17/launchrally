# LaunchRally 0.4.1 — Phase 1 Experimental

LaunchRally 0.4.1 publishes a corrective Phase 1 candidate on npm's non-stable
`experimental` channel. It improves the Human Audit, Init, and Verify journeys,
including authenticated verification, safer route classification, clearer
previews, and actionable completion handoffs.

At publication time, this exact Phase 1 candidate remains **Product Incomplete**
until its independent external verification through separate clean CLI, Codex,
and Claude journeys is reviewed and linked. The release is **Experimental**,
**not P1 Validated**, and **not Stable**.
Publication, CI, downloads, and elapsed time do not change those states.

Phase 0 remains independently Product Complete, P0 Validated, and Stable at
0.3.2. npm `latest` continues to resolve to 0.3.2; 0.4.1 is available only from
`experimental` or by exact version.

Review the [0.4.1 changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md), [migration and failed-release
guidance](p1-migration-notes.md), and [external verification
record](experimental-0.4.1-p1-evidence.md) before adoption.
